Online PDF to Excel converter

DataForge AI is built for invoices, product lists, reports, catalogs, and other PDF documents that need to become spreadsheet data. Upload a text-based PDF and export the detected tables to Excel without manual copy-paste.

The current version works best with machine-readable PDFs. Scanned documents need OCR and are not yet supported. Always review the converted file before financial, legal, or operational use.
